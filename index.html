Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in the BMI Calculator application ("the App").

1. Information Collection and Use

The BMI Calculator app is designed to calculate your Body Mass Index based on the physical attributes (such as height, weight, age, and gender) you input. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of this personal data to our servers or any third-party services. All calculations are performed entirely locally on your device.

2. Data Storage

Any data you enter into the App (e.g., your height or weight) may be stored locally on your device to improve your user experience (such as remembering your last input). This data remains exclusively on your device and is never uploaded, shared, or backed up to external servers by the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. We do not use third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising frameworks that harvest user data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Given that all data is processed locally on the device, no data from children or any other users is transmitted to or stored by us.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us at the email provided on our Google Play Store listing.
